Privacy First & 100% Offline-Friendly

1. Privacy Policy Overview

CeylonSteps ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your personal privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how we handle user data, location permissions, and third-party integrations.

CeylonSteps is built with an offline-first and privacy-focused architecture. Your personal trip memories, journals, routes, photos, and video recordings remain strictly on your local device storage unless you explicitly choose to back them up to your private Google Drive account.

2. Data Collection and Usage

  • Location Information: CeylonSteps requires access to your device's location (GPS) solely to pin your real-time travel stops, calculate distances from your home base, and plot your travel paths on OpenStreetMap. Location data is stored locally in your device's secure database and is never transmitted to any external advertising servers.
  • Device Photos & Videos: When you attach photos or videos to your trip timeline, the app copies them to private internal storage. We do not access, scan, or analyze any media files outside of the ones you explicitly select.
  • Google Account Information: When signing in with Google, we access your basic public profile (display name, email address, and profile photo) solely to personalize your local user profile.

3. Google Drive Data Access (`drive.appdata`)

CeylonSteps utilizes the Google Drive API specifically for cloud backup and recovery services:

  • We request access only to the Hidden AppData Folder (`DriveScopes.DRIVE_APPDATA`).
  • The app cannot read, modify, view, or delete any of your personal files, spreadsheets, or documents stored in your Google Drive.
  • All backup files created by CeylonSteps are stored inside this private application folder and remain entirely under your control.

4. Third-Party Services

The app integrates with the following reliable platforms:

  • OpenStreetMap (osmdroid): For loading interactive maps and offline caching.
  • Google Play Services: For secure OAuth authentication and Google Drive backup synchronization.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

You have full ownership of your data. You can delete individual trips, stops, photos, or the entire local database directly within the app settings. Removing the app's backup archive from your Google Drive completely erases all cloud-stored memories.

6. Terms of Service

  • Permitted Use: CeylonSteps is provided for personal travel planning, route logging, and journaling.
  • Accuracy of Travel Data: Map trails, coordinates, and destination distances are provided for informational and recreational purposes. Always verify road safety and local guidelines while traveling.
  • Modifications: We reserve the right to modify these terms as needed to reflect platform updates or legal requirements.
