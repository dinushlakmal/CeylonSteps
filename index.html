Built for True Island Explorers

Engineered to work seamlessly whether you are climbing Knuckles ranges or unwinding along the southern coastline.

🗺️

Offline OpenStreetMap Engine

Tile caching architecture via osmdroid ensures high-resolution terrain, road paths, and trail maps load with zero active cellular signal.

📍

3D Photo Pins & Glowing Trails

Turn raw GPS coordinates into vivid travel journeys. View stops anchored with realistic 3D photo markers linked by animated route lines.

☁️

Private Google Drive Sync

Backup all travel logs, SQLite databases, and photos safely to your hidden Google Drive AppData space with zero third-party data collection.

⏱️

Multi-Stop Timeline & Journal

Log departure marks, scenic viewpoints, meals, and stays with automatic live distance and time tracking from your home base.

🏛️

100+ Pre-Seeded Lanka Gems

Built-in travel directory of waterfalls, heritage ruins, surf spots, and peaks organized by province with one-tap routing.

📅

Visual Trip Calendar

Organize completed travels and plan upcoming adventures chronologically with month-by-month itinerary previews.

🛡️ Privacy-by-Design Architecture

CeylonSteps runs entirely on-device using local SQLite (Room DB) and Google OAuth2 AppData storage. Your GPS data and personal photos never touch third-party servers.
