🇱🇰 CeylonSteps

Sri Lanka Travel Journal & Map Companion

Privacy First & 100% Offline Architecture

Last updated: September 1, 2026

1. Privacy Commitment

CeylonSteps is built with an offline-first privacy model. Your trip logs, GPS coordinates, photos, and personal memories remain strictly on your local device unless you explicitly initiate a cloud backup to your private Google Drive account.

2. Data Collection & Permissions

  • Precise Location (GPS): Used solely to log travel stops, draw routes on OpenStreetMap, and calculate distances from your home base. Location data is never transmitted to any advertising servers.
  • Photos & Videos: Selected media files are imported directly into the application's private internal storage for timeline rendering.
  • Google Profile: Basic identity data (display name, email, avatar) is used locally to personalize your profile upon Google Sign-In.

3. Google Drive Data Access (drive.appdata)

CeylonSteps requests access strictly to the dedicated Hidden AppData Folder (`DriveScopes.DRIVE_APPDATA`):

  • The app cannot view, read, or edit any of your personal Drive files, folders, or Google Docs.
  • All backup archives (`.zip`) created by CeylonSteps reside solely inside this restricted folder and remain fully under your control.

4. Third-Party Libraries

  • OpenStreetMap (osmdroid): Map tiles and offline caching.
  • Google Play Services: Authentication and AppData backup transmission.

5. Terms of Service

  • CeylonSteps is provided for personal travel journaling and route tracking.
  • Route calculations and map markers are provided for recreational reference. Always exercise personal caution and verify local travel conditions.
